Wire
MPT-10 and MPT-15 use 4P × AWG34 Teflon twisted-pair cable, while all other sizes use 4P
× AWG32 Teflon twisted-pair cable. Pad Connector
Pads under the connector (model suffix: PC) require manual soldering to the corresponding
pins. Glue fixation is recommended.
